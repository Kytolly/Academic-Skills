# Transformers are Sample-Efficient World Models (IRIS)

Resource: interactive world-model literature

Year: 2022

Venue: arXiv / ICLR-era work

Source Verified: Partial

Discovery Source: Citation chasing

## Research Problem

Can a transformer learn tokenized game dynamics and support sample-efficient control?

## Field Position

Autoregressive token-world-model bridge between Dreamer-style latent dynamics and transformer video models.

## Assumption

Discrete image tokens and action-conditioned sequence prediction preserve control-relevant dynamics.

## Method

Image tokenizer, autoregressive transformer world model, and agent learning from imagined rollouts.

## Input/Output

Input: tokenized frames and actions. Output: next tokens and learned behavior.

## Evaluation

Atari 100k sample-efficiency setting.

## Contribution Type

- model
- method

## Limitation

Low-resolution Atari scale and agent-centric evaluation.
