## Research Problem

What efficiency paradigms, architectures, and inference techniques are needed to turn video generators into practical real-time world simulators?

## Field Position

Survey/overview seed for the video-generation route, connecting diffusion, autoregressive and hybrid models to deployment requirements.

## Assumption

Video models can express world dynamics, but computational cost, memory, long context, and inference latency are fundamental barriers to interactive use.

## Method

Taxonomy across efficient modeling paradigms, network architectures, and inference algorithms, including distillation, VAE design, memory, efficient attention, caching, pruning, quantization, and parallelism.

## Input/Output

Input: literature on video generation and video-based world models.

Output: efficiency taxonomy and research agenda.
